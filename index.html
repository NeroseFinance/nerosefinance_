Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

Nerose Finance ("Nerose", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account details you provide, such as your name and email address
  • Transaction and budget data you enter or connect to track your finances
  • Basic device and usage information to keep the app secure and improve performance

How we use it

  • To operate core features like budgets, goals, and spending insights
  • To send account-related notifications you opt into
  • To detect fraud and keep your account secure

What we don't do

We don't sell your personal financial data to advertisers or third parties.

Your choices

You can export or delete your data at any time from Account Settings, or by contacting us at privacy@nerose.store.

Security

How we protect your data

Keeping your financial data safe is core to how we build Nerose.

Encryption

  • Data is encrypted in transit using TLS
  • Sensitive data is encrypted at rest

Account protection

  • Optional two-factor authentication for sign-in
  • Automatic session sign-out on shared or inactive devices
  • Real-time alerts for unusual account activity

Infrastructure

Our systems undergo regular security reviews, and access to customer data is limited to what's needed to operate and support the product.
